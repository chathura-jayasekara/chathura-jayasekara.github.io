About

Engineering, teaching and research

An interdisciplinary professional background connecting mechanical engineering, control and automation, university education, and biomedical engineering research.

My background combines engineering practice with university teaching and research. My engineering experience includes industrial automation, control systems, project coordination, installation, commissioning, and technical work.

I have taught engineering subjects at university level, including engineering mechanics, control systems, vehicle technology, CAD, and other technology-focused subjects.

More recently, my work in Finland has focused on biomedical engineering and quantitative analysis of physiological signals and imaging data.

Education MSc Biomedical Engineering · University of Oulu
Engineering foundation BSc Engineering · Mechanical Engineering, Control & Automation
Additional specialization Postgraduate Diploma in Industrial Automation
