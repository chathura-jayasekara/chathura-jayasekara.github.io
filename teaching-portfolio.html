University Teaching

Teaching Portfolio

A detailed overview of my teaching philosophy, teaching strategies, university teaching experience, pedagogical development, and approach to engineering education.

1. Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective teaching extends beyond content delivery. A teacher should create a learning environment where students are encouraged to explore, question, and apply knowledge in meaningful ways.

Supporting student engagement, motivation, and well-being is equally important. I aim to provide guidance while encouraging students to develop independent learning skills and confidence in applying their knowledge.

My teaching approach combines active learning with structured explanations. I typically begin a topic with a simple, general example that introduces the concept, followed by theoretical explanations and real-world, subject-related examples.

Through interactive discussion and practical activities, students are encouraged to participate actively and apply the concepts they have learned.

Ultimately, my goal is to develop not only students' subject knowledge but also the skills, confidence, and mindset they need for their academic and professional careers.

2. Teaching Strategies & Methods

Pre-Lecture Preparation

I provide students with pre-lecture materials, including reference videos and lecture slides with key points, through Moodle. This allows students to prepare in advance and supports different learning preferences.

Active Note-Taking

During lectures, students are encouraged to develop their own notes as the material is progressively presented. This promotes active engagement, critical thinking, and deeper processing of the material.

Real-World Examples and Question-Based Learning

I use real-world examples to introduce topics and provide practical context. These examples are followed by structured explanations and question-based problems that encourage students to apply the concepts they have learned.

Tutor Sessions and Practical Support

For subjects involving extensive calculations, I conduct additional tutor sessions with smaller groups to provide focused guidance and help students work through problems effectively.

3. Assessment & Feedback

I view assessment not only as a means of measuring knowledge but also as an opportunity to reinforce learning through practical application.

Formative feedback helps students identify areas for improvement, reflect on their learning, and develop stronger problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.

4. Student Support & Engagement

I maintain an open and approachable teaching style, providing guidance and supporting students in navigating both course content and university learning environments.

I have also used Moodle discussion forums to provide opportunities for students to ask questions, participate in collaborative discussions, and support peer-to-peer learning.

5. Pedagogical Training & Development

I completed university-level lecturer training covering teaching and learning methods, student support, curriculum development, ICT in higher education, and university-level educational practices.

I continue to develop my teaching through reflection, experience, and exploration of student-centered learning approaches.

6. Future Development as a Teacher

I aim to further develop my teaching through interdisciplinary approaches, particularly by connecting engineering, biomedical data science, and applied computational analysis.

I am also interested in developing student-centered and blended teaching approaches and contributing to modern engineering education environments.
